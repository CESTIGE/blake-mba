## Portrait exception

No freely reusable Zhang Yiming headshot was found in Wikimedia Commons during this pass. The available Wikipedia thumbnail is a copyrighted magazine cover, and Reuters/Getty results require a separate license. The page therefore uses a clearly labeled typographic avatar for Zhang Yiming instead of copying a restricted portrait.
